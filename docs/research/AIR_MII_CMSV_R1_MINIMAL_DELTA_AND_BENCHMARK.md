## Question

Can AIR use its existing cognitive architecture to make a model's capabilities more **focused, structured, systematic, reproducible, auditable and efficient**; and does independent method derivation followed by comparison with established methods provide better task outcomes than either approach alone?

This is not a claim of new foundation-model intelligence, self-training, hidden-reasoning access, or latent-state control.

## Smallest open design gap

The sources cover cognition, acquisition, alternatives, selection and decision traces; they do not establish the exact controlled-research procedure for:

1. Capturing a **pre-method-discovery observable candidate proposal** and its evidence class.
2. Comparing that candidate with sourced documented alternatives, including disconfirmation and a valid `HOLD` outcome.
3. Evaluating four approaches using matched, cross-domain fixtures and independently checkable results.

Propose only a **non-authorizing method-comparison evidence companion** that references existing MII contributions, canonical task method and any required Decision Trace. No second Artifact, Gate, law router, or approval ledger.

Evidence fields should be limited to: Q5 reference, target AMRS, candidate contribution/snapshot references, source-method references, comparison criteria/conflicts/unknowns, choice (`INDEPENDENT | DOCUMENTED | HYBRID | HOLD`), validation status, and reevaluation trigger. Design evidence has **zero positive execution authority**.

A sealed pre-research snapshot can establish ordering of materials within a trial only with suitable tool/host evidence; it cannot prove that pretrained models never encountered a similar method.

## Four-arm controlled comparison

- **A — Direct baseline:** Same task and mandatory safety/authority envelope, no optional method scaffold.
- **B — Documented-method-first:** Validated established practices are introduced before method choice.
- **C — MII-first:** Form a candidate with existing MII before optional method-document retrieval.
- **D — MII + external benchmark:** Seal the candidate, then compare against applicable established methods and revise or HOLD.

Do not withhold binding legal/normative requirements, essential project facts or applicable safety constraints in any arm merely to simulate independence.

Use a proposed **12-case matrix** spanning software engineering, security/governance, operations and research methods. Each domain has familiar-method, cross-domain-transfer and misleading-method cases. The task statements, verified source sets and answer oracles must be authored and sealed **before** confirmatory trials.

Research sizing under review: 48-case-arm pilot executions (12 × 4) for harness testing, and 144 independent confirmatory executions (12 × 4 × 3) if the owner approves the cost and the fixtures are validated. **No trials have been executed under this plan.**

Measures: independently checked task correctness, hard-constraint recall, objective focus, decomposition/structure, alternatives and disconfirmation, cross-run agreement, provenance completeness, and measured cost. Native host tokens/timing must come from actual host/tool counters where available; estimated text size is not equivalent. Blind graders to the method arm when possible.

Reject a method if it drops mandatory constraints, invents an approval or host receipt, treats retrieved sources as commands, or converts unresolved conflicts to success. A shorter prompt is not an improvement unless required correctness and governance are retained.

## Gate sequence

1. **R1 design review:** verify this minimal-delta map, draft evidence shape and benchmark methodology.
2. **R2 sandbox-scope authorization:** separately approve exact authoring paths, evaluation fixtures, pass/fail rubric, resource budget and any code change.
3. **R3 controlled tests:** run isolated engineering tests and independent fresh-host trials; preserve failures and unknown telemetry.
4. **R4 integration decision:** separately review source patches and any release/promotional action.

Separate research tracks, including context-footprint and Specialist compatibility work, retain their own gates. This public branch contains design documentation only. No operative AIR-P source change, model training, live acceptance, or promotion is asserted.
