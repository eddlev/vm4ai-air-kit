## Minimal proposed delta

AIR's existing cognitive routes and method contracts already support generating, challenging and validating candidate methods. The unresolved research design question is whether an independently documented pre-retrieval method proposal, later exposed to established knowledge and judged against verified outcomes, makes the underlying model's responses more focused, structured, systematic, reproducible, auditable and efficient.

Add **no** new cognitive route, permanent Specialist, universal web search, or second execution authority. Use only a compact nonauthorizing experiment/evidence record and an independently graded comparison. Candidate contributions stay outside operative Artifact/approval state until existing law permits validated ingestion.

## Four experimental arms

| Arm | Input/exposure | Testable difference |
|---|---|---|
| A | Direct model with common constraints, no optional scaffold | Baseline task outcome |
| B | Domain method comparator before selection | Effect of documented-method-first guidance |
| C | Existing MII-style guidance without optional method card | Independent proposal under trial-level document ordering |
| D | Same MII proposal first, externally captured visible snapshot, then method comparator | Increment from post-derivation comparison |

"Independent" means optional document exposure is ordered and observable within a trial. It **does not** mean pretraining lacked related human knowledge or that hidden cognition can be inspected. Mandatory laws, facts and safety requirements remain available in every arm.

## Fixture design and validity limits

- Twelve research-only, fictional cases: three challenge classes (familiar method, cross-domain transfer, misleading-method trap) across software engineering, security/GRC, operations, and research methodology.
- Deterministic outcome oracles include source identity, constrained scheduling, deadline capacity, control/evidence scope and contradictory research measurements.
- The separately sealed target-model inputs exclude grading oracles. A dedicated run-specific input is exposed for each case/arm, and D has two sequential phases.
- This R2 design's method cards are **experimenter-authored candidate summaries with external evidence curation pending**. They are not an authenticated representation of field-tested methods, so superiority over established practice is **not** currently a supportable claim.
- Offline harness calibration tests are checks of fixture consistency and negative-control detection, not GPT-6 live acceptance or proof that MII was actually applied.

## Trial plans (not executed)

Pilot: 12 cases × 4 arms × 1 fresh session = 48 planned trials, used only to calibrate instruments and detect ceiling/floor effects. Proposed confirmation: 12 × 4 × 3 independent repetitions = 144 trials, contingent on owner budget and preregistered hypotheses, external source curation, independently verified rubric and isolation controls.

Hard-stop on an unsupported authorization PASS, missing mandatory constraint, unverified Specialist activation, tool/source identity error, method-card exposure before D's pre-discovery snapshot, or grading-oracle leakage.

Score case-specific independently checkable correctness and required-constraint fidelity first; secondary dimensions are focus, structure, systematic alternatives, repeatability, provenance traceability, and measured efficiency. Constraint-ID mentions alone are not proof of substantive compliance. Native token/time telemetry may only be reported when independently observed; otherwise `UNOBSERVED`.
